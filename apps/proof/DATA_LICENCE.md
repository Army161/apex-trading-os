# Market data licence

**No provider has been chosen, and Proof has no market data of its own yet.**

The data the personal Apex app uses is licensed for personal use. It cannot back a paid product, and Proof does not read it. Until a commercial plan is signed, Proof runs no check for anyone outside (docs/design/12 section 8 and launch stage 5b).

What the plan has to allow:

- Running computations for end users and showing derived statistics: returns, counts, drawdowns, an equity curve.
- US stocks and ETFs daily for at least 10 years and intraday for at least 2, and the major crypto pairs.
- Clear terms on derived data.

Proof never returns raw price series through a page or its API, whatever the plan allows.

When a plan is chosen, record here: the provider, the plan, the date, what the terms allow and forbid, and where the signed terms are kept. Choosing it is a cost Jeremy approves.
