What a check does

1

Extracts the rules

Entry, stop, exit and filters, each linked to the sentence it came from. Anything it had to assume is listed and you can change it.
2

Runs six stress checks

Enough trades. Beats 95% of random traders. Profitable on a locked recent window. Survives acting one bar late, doubled costs, and costs under half the gains.
3

Compares with holding

A rule has to beat buying and holding the same market on risk, not just make money in a rising one.
4

Counts every try

Tweaking until something passes is how luck gets sold as skill. Every re-run is counted and the result is marked down for it.
